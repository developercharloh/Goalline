---
name: Community direction
description: User decisions for ChatBase and account access.
---

ChatBase should be a community like WhatsApp where people chat with others, not an AI assistant. Do not request an OpenAI API key for it.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected repeated AI-key requests and asked for a people-to-people community.

**How to apply:** Keep chat independent of AI services. The user asked to remove login and sign-up gates until they confirm the app works well; do not re-enable account gates without their direction.
